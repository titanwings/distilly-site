02 · WHY DISTILLY

Trust is a product
surface.

A good memory system should show its work. Distilly makes the quiet, useful parts legible.

A/01

Claims, not vibes

Structured facts with confidence, evidence links, and a clear lifecycle from candidate to current.

SEMANTIC TRUTH
A/02
